## Problem

Before or alongside code changes, show how you searched for defects across the inherited system. The reviewer should be able to see whether you found the important boundaries, even if you did not implement every fix inside the timebox.

## Standard

List at least five suspected defects or operational risks. Severity-rank them. For each one, include evidence, impact, and your decision: fixed now, delegated, deferred, or rejected after investigation.

Do not paste generic AI guesses. Ground each item in a file, test, fixture, command, PR diff, screenshot, queue/log evidence, or explicit reproduction.
